# Privacy Policy for FintechX Worklog Sync

**Effective Date:** 2026.07.08.

Thank you for using **FintechX Worklog Sync** (hereinafter referred to as "the Extension"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles your data, specifically regarding your Jira and Clockify credentials.

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide its core functionality, the Extension requires you to input certain authentication details:

- **Jira API Token**
- **Clockify API Key**
- **Email address** associated with your Jira account.

## 2. How We Store and Protect Your Information

- **Local Storage Only:** All credentials are stored strictly locally on your device using the Google Chrome extension storage API (`chrome.storage.sync`).
- **No Developer Access:** We (the developers of the Extension) do **not** have access to your API keys, email address, or any task/time-tracking data.
- **No External Servers:** The Extension does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or authentication data on any external servers owned or operated by us.

## 3. How Your Information is Used

The data you provide is used **solely** for the intended functionality of the Extension:

- To authenticate and establish a secure, direct connection between your browser and the official **Atlassian/Jira APIs** and **Clockify APIs**.
- To securely synchronize time entries recorded in Clockify onto your designated Jira tasks using the provided API tokens.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information or API keys with any third parties.

- Your data is never used for advertising, marketing, or tracking purposes.
- Communication occurs exclusively between your local browser and the official third-party services (Jira and Clockify) you have authorized.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Extension interacts directly with third-party platforms (Jira and Clockify). Their use of data is governed by their respective privacy policies:

- [Atlassian / Jira Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- [Clockify Privacy Policy](https://clockify.me/privacy)

## 6. Your Rights and Data Control

You retain 100% control over your data at all times. You can delete or clear your stored API keys and email address at any time by:

- Using the "Adatok törlése" (Clear Data) button within the Extension
- Removing or uninstalling the Extension from your browser (e.g., Google Chrome), which will automatically erase all associated locally stored data.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
